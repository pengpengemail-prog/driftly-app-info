Support

Driftly • Last updated: April 26, 2026

Driftly helps people settle into a calmer evening routine with sleep sounds, breathing sessions, reminders, and premium content.

Common help topics

If bedtime reminders are not appearing, open iPhone Settings and confirm notifications are allowed for Driftly.

If Tomorrow Outlook is missing, confirm location access is allowed for Driftly and try again. The feature may also remain hidden until Apple Weather data and required attribution can be loaded.

If premium access does not appear after purchase, open the paywall and use Restore Purchases while signed in to the same App Store account used for the subscription.

If audio stops unexpectedly, confirm Silent Mode, Focus settings, and your current audio route, then reopen the selected sound from the app library.

Account and subscriptions

Billing, renewal, cancellation, and refunds are managed by Apple through the App Store. Subscription settings can be managed from your Apple account subscriptions screen.
