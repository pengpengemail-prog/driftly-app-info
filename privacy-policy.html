Privacy Policy

Driftly • Last updated: April 26, 2026

This policy describes what Driftly stores on the current device, how permissions are used, and how local data can be removed.

Information stored on device

Driftly stores a limited set of local preferences on this device, including whether onboarding has been completed and which bedtime reminder preset you selected. This version does not create a Driftly account or maintain a personal cloud profile.

Notifications

If you enable bedtime reminders, Driftly requests notification permission so the app can deliver a local notification at the time you select.

Location for Tomorrow Outlook

If you use Tomorrow Outlook, Driftly may request location access while the app is in use. This lets the app automatically read local weather from your current area and adapt tonight's recommendations to tomorrow's conditions without asking you to choose a city.

Managing permissions

You can turn off notification access or revoke location access at any time in iPhone Settings for Driftly. If a permission is withdrawn, the related feature will stop working until you grant access again.

Data not requested by this version

This version of Driftly does not request access to camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or Health data.

Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions and billing are handled by Apple through In-App Purchase. Driftly reads Apple purchase entitlement status only to determine whether premium access should be unlocked or restored on this device. Billing records are managed by Apple under your App Store account.

Data retention and deletion

Local preferences remain on your device until you change them, remove the app, or clear the related system permissions and reminder settings. Because this version does not maintain a Driftly account database, deleting Driftly from the device removes the app's locally stored data from that device.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the app after an update means the revised policy applies to your use of Driftly.
